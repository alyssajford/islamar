Your Home Away from Home

Experience Costa Rican Luxury

Escape to this stunning 3-bedroom, 3-bath villa nestled in the gated Villas Catalinas community along Guanacaste's breathtaking Pacific coast. Soaring vaulted ceilings, ocean-view bedrooms, and a beautifully appointed kitchen make Villa Islamar the perfect retreat for relaxation or adventure.

Step onto private balconies on both floors, wake up to shimmering Pacific views, then stroll down to Playa Prieta or Playa Penca — both just minutes on foot. Playa Flamingo, Playa Potrero, and the gorgeous Playa Pan de Azúcar are a short drive away.

The shared community infinity pool overlooks the ocean and features a BBQ grill and lounge furniture, while nearby restaurants, grocery stores, and local attractions are all within easy reach.
